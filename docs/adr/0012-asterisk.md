# ADR-0012: Asterisk telephony foundation

Status: Accepted historical P00 reservation. At P00, Asterisk was planned and
telephony was not implemented. That implementation-status statement is superseded
by the canonical P11 application-level Asterisk/ARI contract in ADR-0084, ADR-0085
and ADR-0086. Asterisk remains the telephony/media engine; production Asterisk
infrastructure has not been deployed or certified by those application contracts.
P19 signaling governance is defined separately in ADR-0100 and remains PLANNED.
